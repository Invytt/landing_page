## 2. The problem

**Section label:** THE PROBLEM
**Heading:** Hosting today is fragmented and stressful.

Planning an event today means juggling ten different tools. Group chats for RSVPs. Spreadsheets for headcounts. Google searches for vendors. Pinterest for inspiration. UPI requests at midnight to split costs. Guesswork for how much food to order.

It's fragmented, it's stressful — and the host, the person meant to be celebrating with everyone else, ends up doing the most work and enjoying it the least.

**Punchline:** Invytt fixes that. One platform to invite your people, manage who's coming, plan what you need, find the right vendors, and split the cost. No more chaos, just the fun part.

**Optional "before" chip grid (the chaos):**
Group chats · Spreadsheets · Google searches · Pinterest boards · Midnight UPI · Food guesswork

## 3. Five pillars (the platform)

**Section label:** THE PLATFORM
**Heading:** Five pillars. One platform.
**Subhead:** Every part of the hosting journey — from the moment you decide to throw something to the moment the last guest leaves — in a single mobile-first experience.

### Pillar 1 — Guest RSVP Management
*End-to-end guest coordination, from invite to event day.*
- Create & send personalised invitations
- Guestlist tracking & real-time view
- Venue location & directions sharing
- RSVP dashboard — confirmed, pending, declined at a glance
- +1 management per guest
- One shareable RSVP link
- **RSVP over WhatsApp** — guests respond without an app
- Event update notifications & reminder blasts
- Activity timeline (live log)
- Custom guest Q&A
- Payments & cost-splitting

### Pillar 2 — Invitation Design & Templates
*Beautiful, customisable invites that feel personal, not corporate.*
- Template library (event-ready designs)
- Custom design editor — colours, fonts, layout
- Mobile-ready on any screen
- Brand kit for consistent event branding
- Ready in minutes

### Pillar 3 — Smart Inventory Management
*AI-powered supply planning, scaled to your guest count.*
- Inventory list builder
- **AI quantity estimator** — how much food, drink, supplies you actually need
- Guest contribution assignments (who brings what)
- Per-item cost estimates so you never go over budget

### Pillar 4 — Vendor Marketplace
*Discover, compare, and book everything that makes events happen.*
- Vendor discovery — filter by category, rating, price
- Caterers, decorators, photographers, bartenders, DJs, and more
- Booking & enquiry — quotes and confirmations in-app
- Ratings & reviews (verified, community-driven)
- Detailed vendor profiles — portfolio, pricing, availability

### Pillar 5 — Party Planning Management
*The central hub that ties it all together.*
- Task & checklist manager (venue booking → day-of setup)
- Event timeline / run-of-show
- Real-time budget tracking across categories
- Collaboration with co-hosts — shared tasks, stay aligned

## 4. How it works (3 steps)

**Heading:** From idea to "last guest leaves" in one app.

1. **Create your event.** Pick a template, set the date and venue, design your invite. Ready in minutes.
2. **Invite & coordinate.** Share one link, track RSVPs (even over WhatsApp), manage +1s, and split costs automatically.
3. **Plan & book.** Let AI size your supply list, assign who brings what, and book vetted vendors — all without leaving Invytt.

## 10. FAQ

**Q: What is Invytt?**
An all-in-one event and party planning platform — invitations, guest RSVPs, AI inventory planning, a vendor marketplace, and cost-splitting, in a single mobile app.

**Q: How is it different from group chats and spreadsheets?**
Everything lives in one place and talks to each other. Your guest count feeds your inventory estimate, which feeds your budget, which connects to vendors and cost-splitting. No copy-pasting between ten tools.

**Q: Do my guests need to download the app to RSVP?**
No. Share one link, or let guests RSVP directly over WhatsApp — no app required.

**Q: How does the AI inventory estimator work?**
It scales food, drink, and supply quantities to your confirmed guest count, then gives per-item cost estimates so you stay on budget.

**Q: Can I split costs with my guests?**
Yes. Collect contributions and split costs among guests right inside the event — no more midnight UPI requests.

**Q: What kind of vendors can I book?**
Caterers, decorators, photographers, bartenders, DJs, and more — with profiles, pricing, availability, and verified reviews.
